1. ## The Best Of the Best - Chili Octopus

<div class="photo-grid-2">
  <img src="/octopus.png" alt="Chili octopus">
  <img src="/octopus2.jpg" alt="Chili octopus in pieces">
</div>
In Ukulhas you can eat as much seafood as you like. But the best thing we tried, without any doubt, was the chili octopus. Whole (a little tough) or in pieces, it's slightly spicy and very, very delicious. It's basically one big muscle, so it's packed with protein. What could be better?

You can eat spicy octopus in almost every restaurant in Ukulhas. The ones we prefer are [Paguro](https://www.paguromaldives.com/) and [Olhumathi](https://share.google/vdNYOyJJe2wIPOUKO). Prices are good – a portion is around $10.

2. ## Coconuts 
![Coconut](/coconuts.avif)
I'm still not sure if I like them or just love how fancy and exotic they look... but when they're ripe, they're delicious and perfect for very hot days. 

3. ## Passion Fruit Juice
![Passion Fruit](/passionfruit.avif)
I've never tried anything so fresh and tasty – the best juice ever! You can probably get it almost everywhere on the island. This photo is from [Retro Royal](https://share.google/7hNMJ0edUIoz9B9vl)

4. ## Maldivian Style Pizza
![Pizza](/pizza.avif)
What's special about this pizza? It's loaded with seafood, the dough is thin and light, and it's generously topped. I don't know anyone who is not a pizza lover, do you?

Where to eat it - again in [Olhumathi](https://share.google/vdNYOyJJe2wIPOUKO)

A few more interesting foods, drinks and places to try:

- Maldivian style breakfast 
Breakfast was included at our hotel, with 3 options: Maldivian, American (French toast and sausages) and something in between. I always like to try something new, and I really enjoyed the Maldivian one – something like very thin pancakes, an omelette and a sauce made with tomatoes and lots of onion.
![breakfast](/breakfast.avif)

- Shrimp – definitely not for everyone, but an interesting choice when served like this, on a skewer.
![shrimps](/shrimps.avif)

- Sweets – I wouldn't call these typical Maldivian fruits, except for the small bananas. This is the dessert we ordered at [Olhumathi](https://share.google/vdNYOyJJe2wIPOUKO) fruit, a chocolate "salami" and a red rolled sweet that tastes like candy.
![sweets](/sweets.avif)

- And here is our improvised Christmas Eve dinner - some of the food we brought with us, some we bought at the nearest shop and some we ordered at the hotel.
![christmaseve](/christmaseve.avif)

- And the best Christmas dinner - at [Celeste Restaurant](https://www.google.com/maps/place//data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x3b4057c59f7a3a55:0xd59520f0cd8b8dfb?source=g.page.share) - a buffet with everything you can imagine; we ate way too much, and my favourite was the chocolate mousse 🍫 
![christmaseve](/christmas.avif)

And one last fact worth knowing: on local islands like Ukulhas there is NO alcohol, because Islam is the official religion of the Maldives. Alcohol is only served at resort islands and on boats, so we drank non-alcoholic ginger beer. But honestly – the sun and the ocean are intoxicating enough.
